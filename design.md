# Karnala — camera viewfinder

The actual panorama is the main surface. Compact sans headings and monospaced position labels replace the large ivory editorial title. A centered vertical orientation rail, charcoal control dock, lime selection, and dark observation drawer give the viewer a camera identity rather than a paper notebook. Six presets still mean six angles within one photograph, never six invented locations.

The original image, camera coordinates, observation index, saved viewpoints, and validated share fragments stay intact. Pointer drag and touch manipulation belong to the panorama engine. Wheel zoom applies only on the panorama; controls and notebook scroll remain native. The camera readout and zoom range expose real engine state. Keyboard and touch use the existing native controls and dialogs.

Palette and typography tokens live in `src/index.css`. Reduced motion remains live and stops an in-flight orientation transition at its target. There is no custom cursor, ambient loop, invented map, or new historical claim. Dialogs use contrasting charcoal surfaces and light text; saved notes remain personal and local.
